Digital Citizenship

WHat is digital citizenship?

Digital citizenship refers to the responsible and ethical use of technology, particularly the internet, social media, and digital devices. It's essentially about how people, especially students, should behave, interact, and protect themselves and others while participating in the digital world.

WHat is digital law?

Digital law refers to the legal rights and restrictions governing technology use, particularly regarding actions and behaviors online. It's often considered one of the core components of digital citizenship, since it deals with the legal side of navigating the digital world.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It's a document or set of rules that outlines how technology, networks, or internet access should and shouldn't be used within a particular organization, school, workplace, or institution.

What is plagiarism?

Plagiarism is the act of using someone else's work, ideas, or words and presenting them as your own without giving proper credit to the original source.

What is academic dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty (also called academic misconduct) refers to any form of cheating, dishonesty, or fraud committed in an academic setting that violates a school's or institution's code of conduct. It undermines the integrity of the learning and evaluation process.

What are the rules for AI generated content?

AI generated content may be used only when allowed and only in responsible ways
