Adults

Resources for Adult Players

It's never too late to get back on the pitch. Discover Soccer UT connects adult players across the Wasatch Front with fields, leagues, and pickup games that fit busy schedules, whether you're a former college player or someone who just wants to kick the ball around after work.

Most adult leagues in Utah run in seasonal sessions, with games typically held on weekday evenings or weekends. Teams are usually grouped by skill level, so everyone gets a fun and competitive match.

How to Join a League

  1. Decide which skill level fits you best: recreational, intermediate, or competitive.
  2. Choose a league format, such as 11-a-side, 7-a-side, or indoor futsal.
  3. Register as a free agent or sign up with your own team of friends or coworkers.
  4. Pay your registration fee and pick up your team jersey or pinnies.
  5. Show up, warm up, and enjoy your first match!

Don't have a full roster? No problem. Free agents are placed on teams that need extra players, which is a great way to make new friends and get into the game.

Soccer Terms to Know

Pickup Game
An informal, unscheduled match where players show up and split into teams on the spot.
Free Agent
A player who signs up for a league without a team and is assigned to one that needs more players.
Futsal
A fast-paced indoor version of soccer played on a hard court with a smaller, heavier ball.
Shin Guards
Protective gear worn under the socks, required by most leagues for every player.
